URiCA Fall Symposium 2026 Presenters

Comparative Pentaptychs: The Trace in Photography and AI Generated Imagery

Poster Location 1 & 2

Joie Bouy, Art major; Elina Meijer, Art major; and Cameron Rayniak, Art major

Mentor: Teri Frame, Art and Design

Abstract: This research investigates photographic and artificially generated images from across various historical eras, and the comparative application to concepts of reality and how it is expressed through images. More specifically, the images will relate to topics surrounding perspective, bias, truth, post-truth, the trace, objectivity, authenticity, credibility, and The Real. We each will explore these topics by creating three pentaptychs that include the methods of tintype photography, black and white film photography, color film photography, digital photography, and AI-generated imagery. Each investigator will explore the aesthetic use of these methods to express their own ideas in a collaborative effort. This research is significant because it invites each investigator to address individualized content in a collaborative context. It also invites the challenge of utilizing both traditional and emerging imagery, making this research both a technical and conceptual challenge.
