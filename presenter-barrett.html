Evaluation of iPad Pro LiDAR for Diameter at Breast Height Estimation in Deciduous Forests

Poster Location 15

Sabrina Barrett, Environmental Science major

Mentor: Rocio Duchesne, Geography, Geology, and Environmental Science

<

Abstract: Accurate measurements of diameter at breast height (DBH) are essential for forest inventories, biomass estimation, and ecosystem monitoring. Traditional field measurements are reliable but require considerable time and labor, creating interest in mobile LiDAR technologies that provide rapid, non-destructive alternatives. Recent iPad-based LiDAR applications can generate three-dimensional point clouds of trees, but their accuracy depends on both the scanning application and the algorithm used to estimate trunk diameter. This study evaluates three LiDAR scanning applications —Abound (AB), ForestScanner (FS), and 3DScanner(3D)—combined with three geometric fitting algorithms to determine which produces the most accurate DBH estimates relative to field measurements.
