URiCA Fall Symposium 2026 Presenters

Burn This Scenic Design

Poster Location 5

Emma Cordray, Theatre Education major

Mentor: Matthew Imhoff, Theatre and Dance

Abstract: This presentation will explore my scenic design process for Burn This by Lanford Wilson, developed for the University of Wisconsin-Whitewater Theatre/Dance Department. Centered on the conceptual metaphor of "a dancer trapped in a music box," the design examines the tension between confinement and release that defines Anna's emotional journey throughout the play. The loft apartment functions as both a realistic Manhattan living space and a symbolic container for grief, memory, and transformation. The presentation discusses how scenic choices support Wilson's themes of loss, artistic identity, and human connection through a single environment. Industrial architecture, open rehearsal space, and carefully layered personal artifacts create a world that is simultaneously expansive and emotionally restrictive. Influenced by movement vocabulary and dance imagery, the design treats the loft as a choreographic landscape where characters navigate zones of safety, collision, and intimacy. My presentation will highlight how scenic design can function not only as a physical setting, but also as an active storytelling element. In addition to discussing my work on this project, my presentation will also feature process photographs documenting the design journey, and a miniature scenic model, illustrating the final design solution, along with the accompanying design research packet. Together, these materials provide insight into the research, analysis, and creative decision-making that shaped the production's visual world.
