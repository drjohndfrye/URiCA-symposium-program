Muon Detection in the Upper Atmosphere

Poster Location 23

Alex Pollard, Physics and Philosophy major

Abstract: Last year at GWU, the Student Physical Society(SPS) worked through an exciting Undergraduate project where students construct a high-altitude balloon built with housing that houses a scintillator that detects muon decay. While the project wasn't launched last year, the presentation will be about the goals of the project, the science, and the engineering process involved.
