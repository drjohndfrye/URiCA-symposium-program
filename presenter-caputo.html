URiCA Fall Symposium 2026 Presenters

ERBE - Germany Unscripted Travel Study 2026

Poster Location 3

Grace Caputo, Communication and Film Studies major; Eydie Furnett, Journalism major; Claudia (CJ) Van Aken, Electronic Media major

Mentors: Sue Wildermuth, Communication and Amal Ibrahim, Communication

Abstract: Our research explores architecture, artwork, and film in German culture, particularly the Neuschwanstein Castle, German expressionism artwork, and the Camera Obscura, and how it has impacted the world. Through pre production research and planning, production utilizing our phone cameras and microphones, and post production editing on capcut, procreate, and adobe audition, we were able to put together our findings in the form of a 6 minute documentary. Our work showed how the Neuschwanstein Castle inspired Walt Disney and his entire franchise, notably Cinderella and Auroa’s castles at the Diseny Parks. German expressionism can be found in artwork all across Germany, through stickers and graffiti to continue promoting one's political and social ideologies. The camera obscura uses principles that present-day cameras are based upon, making famous camera brands and film franchises possible. Our study is significant in conveying the importance of studying abroad and the unique, cross-cultural outlook it brings to students, and the experience of editing in a fast-paced, new environment whilst on the go.
