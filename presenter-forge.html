URiCA Fall Symposium 2026 Presenters

Busy Bees, Buzzing Brains

Poster Location 25

Dynali Forge, Psychology major

Mentor: Jessica Walz, Sociology, Criminology, and Anthropology

Abstract: URiCA Summer Fellow Dynali Forge and Dr. Jessica Walz formulated a survey-based study titled Busy Bees, Buzzing Brains, to investigate how climate change impacts the mental health of honey beekeepers in Southern Wisconsin. This study aimed to gain a deeper understanding of beekeepers’ perspectives on climate change, how climate change impacts their beekeeping, and how this has ultimately impacted beekeepers’ mental health. Sections of the survey measured participants’ climate awareness, their perceived climate impact, climate impact anxiety, general climate anxiety using the Climate Change Worry Scale (Stewart, 2021), and participant demographics. This research was directed to residents of Southern Wisconsin and to those who have been beekeeping for at least two years. Participant outreach was conducted by visiting farmers markets, contacting local beekeeping associations, digital outreach, and being published in the newsletter Badger Bee, by the Wisconsin Honey Producers Association. Forge and Dr. Walz also traveled to the Wisconsin Honey Convention in Wisconsin Dells for additional participant outreach and networking opportunities. The results of the survey had only 7 valid responses, which leads us to pose this study as a pilot. Due to the lack of participants, we are currently unable to determine generalizable trends. However, based on the correlations from these responses, there was a strong correlation of 0.785 between participants’ perceived climate impact and their climate impact anxiety. There was also a strong correlation of 0.609 between participants’ climate awareness and their climate impact anxiety. Moving forward Forge and Dr. Walz hope to gain more participants to formulate a full study and find generalizable trends.
