URiCA Fall Symposium 2026 Presenters

Breaking Into the Game Industry: Why Conferences and Networking Matter!

Poster Location 0

Katrina Senebouttarath, Media Arts and Game Development major; Kevin King, Computer Science major; Josh Boehmer, Computer Science major; and Drew Schneider, Media Arts and Game Development major

Mentor: Bill Miller, Art and Design

Abstract: Breaking into the video game industry takes more than technical skills and classroom experience. Networking, building professional relationships, and learning from people already working in the industry are just as important. In March 2026, 4 officers from GAMED Club received a travel grant to attend the Game Developers Conference (GDC) in San Francisco, one of the largest annual gatherings of game industry professionals. In this presentation, we will share our individual experiences, what we learned, and how attending GDC helped us grow as aspiring game developers. We will discuss the value of networking, attending industry sessions, connecting with professionals, and exploring opportunities outside the classroom. Additionally, our indie game development team had the opportunity to showcase their game at GDC with sponsorship from the Wisconsin Games Alliance! Our goal is to show how experiences like GDC can help students build industry connections, gain valuable knowledge, and create opportunities for their future careers. We hope to encourage other students to take advantage of events like these and become more involved in the game development community.
